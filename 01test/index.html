Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the practice of using technology safely, responsibly, ethically, and effectively in online spaces-at school, at work, and in everyday life.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is the set of rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital content.

What is AUP?

First of all, it means Acceptable Use Policy. An Acceptible Use Policy is a set of rules that explain how technology, devices, and networks may-and may not-be used within an organization like a school, workplace, or website.

What is Plagarism?

Plagarism is using someone else's work, ideas, or words as your own without giving proper credit.

Even if you do it on accident, it is still plagarism if it doesn't have credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair advantage or misrepresents their learning.

In simple terms: it's cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork.

What are the Rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated Content may be used only when allowed and only in responsible ways. It is a tool for learning, not to skip critical thinking or hard work.
