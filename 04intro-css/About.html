About Earbeans

Welcome to Earbeans! We are a community-driven platform dedicated to breaking people out of their musical ruts. We don't sell hardware—we share human-curated music "beans" to keep your playlists fresh, exciting, and completely free of stale algorithm loops.

What We Are All About

Earbeans is built differently than your typical streaming homepage. Here is what you can expect when you dive into our community:

  • Hidden Gem Discovery: We intentionally bypass the top-40 charts to help you find incredible underground talent.
  • Vibe-Based Recommendations: Our customized quizzes match songs directly to your specific mood and sonic taste.
  • Community Playlists: Collaboration is key. You can swap tracks and build ultimate playlists alongside other members.

The Legend of the Earbean

How did a music platform get named after a legume? Here is the breakdown of our history and terminology:

The Inspiration
Its actually a pretty simple and funny story! Our founder Marlee just... loves to call everything a "bean". Cats, dogs, people, anything that's cute or interesting gets named a "bean"! (And honestly its just a fun word to say lol - Marlee). So hence the name earbeans!
The Mission
What is our mission? To defeat the "Algorithm Trap." Major streaming apps keep feeding you the same five artists. Earbeans was born to plant the seeds for a completely organic music discovery experience. (I'm tired of hearing the same 5 songs at a dance - Marlee).
What is a "Bean" and how do you use it in our website?
On our site, a "Bean" isn't a headphone or a coffee bean—it’s our slang for a spectacular, under-the-radar song recommendation submitted by a real human being. We have a special section where users can submit their favorite hidden gems!

How to Drop and Discover Music in 4 Steps

Ready to join the audio revolution? Getting started with our music-sharing ecosystem is incredibly simple:

  1. Spin the Roulette: Let our algorithm surprise you with a random selection of hidden gems.
  2. Take a Quiz: Run through one of our interactive sound-checkers to find a brand new song, artist, or album.
  3. Pass the Bean: Drop your own favorite under-the-radar tracks into our sharing pool to help someone else find their next obsession.
  4. Give Feedback: Share your thoughts about our website and the recommendations you receive straight to us so we can improve our website.

Get in Touch

Have questions about the platform, or want to host a local music swap? We host our community events and coordinate our physically sourced music recommendations out of our favorite local record shop!
