Who were the Signers?

Famous Signers

The Declaration of Independence was signed by 56 delegates from the thirteen American colonies. Here are some of the most notable signers:

John Hancock (Massachusetts)
President of the Continental Congress and the first to sign the document, leaving a famously large signature.
Thomas Jefferson (Virginia)
The primary author who drafted the document's core arguments for human rights, liberty, and self-governance.
Benjamin Franklin (Pennsylvania)
The oldest signer at age 70, as well as a world-renowned scientist, inventor, and diplomat who helped win the war.
John Adams (Massachusetts)
A passionate voice for independence on the Congress floor who served on the drafting committee with Jefferson.
Samuel Adams (Massachusetts)
A radical political organizer and leader of the Sons of Liberty who helped spark early colonial resistance.

These signers, along with the other delegates, risked their lives and fortunes to declare independence from Britain, setting the stage for the birth of a new nation.
